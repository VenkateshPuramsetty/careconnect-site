Benefits for Elderly Users

  • Receive compassionate support from trained medical students.
  • Maintain independence with a little extra help when needed.
  • Stay socially connected and reduce feelings of isolation.
  • Access to reliable assistance for errands, appointments, and companionship.

Benefits for Remote Family Members

  • Peace of mind knowing loved ones have reliable local support.
  • Reduced stress about managing daily needs from afar.
  • Real-time updates and communication with volunteers.
  • Encourages community-driven care for elderly relatives.

How It Works

1. Registration

Elderly individuals and medical students sign up on our platform with basic details.

2. Matching

We carefully match elderly users with nearby medical student volunteers based on location and preferences.

3. Coordination

Volunteers and families coordinate tasks like companionship, check-ins, errands, and basic support.

4. Ongoing Support

Stay connected with real-time updates, feedback, and flexible scheduling options.
